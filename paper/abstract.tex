\begin{abstract}
The Philippines recognizes the importance of science and technology and their roles in research and development, invention, and innovation, and consequently in the generation, utilization, and transfer of intellectual property. There are Technology Transfer Offices (TTOs) that were established to facilitate the protection and management of IP-generating research in universities, one of which is the Technology Transfer and Business Development Office (TTBDO) of UPV. At present, the TTBDO uses various third-party software tools and services to record disclosures, store documents, and track IP cases. The setup leads to fragmented workflows and potential risks such as incomplete records and missed deadlines. In light of these issues, the study proposes and develops a prototype web-based management information system that streamlines TTBDO's operations for overseeing and managing the IP portfolio, as well as facilitating the application of intellectual property rights by technology generators. The system is developed following an Agile software development life cycle, with requirements gathered through interviews and document analysis. Although developed in the context of UPV, the system is envisioned to be scalable to other UP constituent universities and similar academic institutions in the Philippines.
