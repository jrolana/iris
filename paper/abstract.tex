\begin{abstract}
In the Philippines, there are Technology Transfer Offices (TTOs) that were established to facilitate the protection and management of IP-generating research in universities, one of which is the Technology Transfer and Business Development Office (TTBDO) of UPV. At present, the TTBDO uses various third-party software tools and services to record disclosures, store documents, and track IP cases. The setup leads to fragmented workflows and potential risks, including incomplete records and missed deadlines. In light of these issues, the study proposes and develops a prototype web-based management information system that streamlines TTBDO's operations for overseeing and managing the IP portfolio, and facilitates the application of intellectual property rights by technology generators.  An initial prototype of the system includes features such as role-based dashboards, an intellectual property application registry, portfolio management and analytics view, and user management for admins. The system was developed following an Agile software development life cycle, with requirements gathered through interviews and document analysis. Although developed in the context of UPV, the system is envisioned to be scalable to other UP constituent universities and similar academic institutions in the Philippines.
