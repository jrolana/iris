\chapter{Non-Functional Requirements Tables}
\label{sec:appendixb}

This appendix contains the non-functional requirements (NFRs) for IRIS, organized by category. These tables specify system qualities, constraints, and standards that served as the basis for designing and evaluating IRIS in terms of performance, security, reliability, usability, maintainability, scalability, and compliance.
