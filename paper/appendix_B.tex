\chapter{Non-Functional Requirements Tables}
\label{sec:appendixb}

This appendix contains the complete set of non-functional requirements (NFRs) for IRIS, organized by category. These tables specify system qualities, constraints, and standards that ensure IRIS operates efficiently, securely, and reliably while meeting usability and compliance standards.

\hline
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Requirement} \\
