\section{Conclusion}

\section{Recommendation}
For future research and development, it is recommended to implement a subsystem that integrates with IPOPHL to further automate the TTBDO's operations.
From the interview, the TTBDO expressed frustrations with IPOPHL's delayed notification of application publishment, and therefore requires manual checking of the IPOPHL website to monitor the status of applications.
By developing an automated subsystem that interfaces with IPOPHL, the TTBDO can receive timely updates on application statuses, allowing for quicker responses and improved workflow management.
Furthermore, this subsystem should include a payment management feature that handles the payment tracking and processing for the TTBDO, thus minimizing the possibility of errors such as delays in payment.

Additionally, it is recommended to create a headless content management system (CMS) that can be used by the TTBDO, especially the non-technical users, to manage and update their website data and content more efficiently.
A headless CMS allows the TTBDO to easily modify data and content that might change over the years without needing to directly interact with the website's codebase, thus, making it more user-friendly for non-technical users.
These data include but are not limited to: contact information, office hours, list of colleges, document forms, IP statuses, IP application flow, and other relevant information that the TTBDO needs to regularly update on their website.

To further centralize the entire application workflow, it is recommended to create a workspace subsystem where the collaborators and the admin can effectively communicate on a single platform.
This workspace can include features such as a chat system, file sharing, and task management to facilitate better communication and collaboration among the TTBDO members and the Technology Generators.

The testing conducted for this research was limited to the knowledge of the researchers, their advisers, and the TTBDO. 
For future research, it is recommended to conduct a more extensive testing with a larger pool of users, including potential Technology Generators who will be using the system in the future.
Furthermore, professional testing can also be conducted to ensure the system's functionality, usability, and security are up to industry standards.

Finally, it is recommended to create a mobile application for the system to further enhance the accessibility of the system for the users, especially for the Technology Generators who may not have access to a computer at all times.
A mobile application provides convenience, flexibility, and accessibility for users to access the system on-the-go, allowing them to stay updated on their applications any time, anywhere.
