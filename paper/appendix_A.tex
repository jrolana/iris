\chapter{Functional Requirements Tables}
\label{sec:appendixa}

This appendix contains the complete set of functional requirements (FR) for IRIS, organized by the eight key functional areas. These tables provide detailed descriptions of the specific system behaviors, capabilities, and interactions necessary to address the inefficiencies of TTBDO’s current manual IP management process. They serve as a reference to guide the design and implementation of the system.
