\appendix

\chapter{Functional Requirements Tables}
\label{sec:appendixa}

This appendix presents the functional requirements (FRs) that served as the basis for the design and implementation of IRIS. The requirements are organized according to the main system capabilities needed to support intellectual property application management, role-based access, document handling, monitoring, reporting, and public access. These requirements define the expected system functions at a general level and do not necessarily describe every interface element or implementation detail presented in Chapter 4.

\section{Authentication and Role-Based Access}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\caption{Authentication and Role-Based Access}
\label{tab:fr_auth_roles}
\begin{tabular}{|p{2.2cm}|p{12cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Requirement} \\
\hline
FR-01 & The system shall require registered users to authenticate before accessing internal system features. \\
\hline
FR-02 & The system shall implement role-based access control for Technology Generators, TTBDO Admins, UP Officials, and Guests. \\
\hline
FR-03 & The system shall allow administrators to manage user access through approval, invitation, and role assignment. \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
